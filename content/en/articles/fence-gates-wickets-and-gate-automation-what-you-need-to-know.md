# Fence Gates, Wickets and Gate Automation – What You Need to Know?

Fence gates, wickets and gate automation are among the most important elements that keep your yard safe and private. Even the most modern security cameras cannot replace properly chosen and installed gates, so the choice deserves careful thought.  
To make the process easier, we go through the most important things to know before choosing gates, wickets and automation.

<!--more-->

---

## Fence Gates and Wickets – What to Consider?

Gates are installed rarely, so it is natural that many plot owners do not think through every detail. The most common mistake is **underestimating the width of the driveway or walkway**.

### 1. Driveway Width

Before installing gates, decide how wide the driveway needs to be.  
If:

- the yard is small,
- there is little room to park a car,

— it is worth choosing a **wider driveway** so that driving in is comfortable.

The chosen width also determines the gate type:

- **sliding gates**,
- **swing gates**.

---

## When to Choose Sliding Gates?

If you are planning a **wide driveway (e.g. 5 m)**, sliding gates are recommended.  
They:

- do not take up space in the yard when opening,
- can be longer and sturdier.

However, make sure there is enough room next to the fence for the gate to slide.  
Sliding gates need **about one and a half times their length** in free space (gate length × 1.5).  
For example, 5 m gates need a **free zone of about 7.5 m** along the fence.

Important: the area along the fence must not be planted or built on.

---

## When to Choose Swing Gates?

If the yard is spacious and you prefer a classic design, **swing gates** are a great fit.  
They can open:

- inwards,
- outwards,
- mixed (one leaf inwards, the other outwards).

The standard driveway width is **3–4 metres**.  
However, make sure the gate opening zone is completely clear:

- no plants,
- no cars,
- no yard items,
- no pedestrian traffic.

---

## Fence Posts – the Basis of Durability

For gates to last a long time, the posts must be installed properly.  
Posts must be:

- set in concrete **about 60 cm deep**,
- stable and able to withstand loads,
- suited to the specific gate type.

This ensures the gates will withstand wind, weight and intensive use.

---

## What to Know Before Choosing Gate Automation?

Gate automation is usually planned at the same time as the gates are installed, because the **cables have to be laid in advance**.

Automation can be installed on:

- **sliding gates**,
- **swing gates**.

### Automation Control Options

Modern technology lets you control your gates:

- with a remote control,
- with a phone call,
- with a mobile app.

For these functions to work, additional modules are needed:

- **GSM module** – to control the gates by phone,
- **Wi‑Fi module** – to control them with an app.

These solutions must be planned **at the automation installation stage** so that the cables can be laid and the equipment prepared properly.

---

## Conclusion

Fence gates, wickets and automation are key elements of yard security.  
By choosing the right gate type, driveway width, post construction and automation control, you get a convenient, safe and long-lasting solution.
